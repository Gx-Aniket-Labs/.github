## About

**GX Aniket Labs** is a student-led developer community focused on software development, practical learning, and collaborative innovation.

We bring together students and developers who want to explore technologies, work on real-world ideas, and develop solutions through hands-on experience.

Our goal is to create an environment where learning goes beyond tutorials and ideas become working software.

## Our Mission

To encourage students and developers to learn by building, collaborate through shared projects, and grow their technical skills through practical experience.

Our work is guided by four principles:

| Principle | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Innovation** | Exploring ideas and developing useful solutions |
| **Collaboration** | Working together and sharing knowledge |
| **Learning** | Developing skills through hands-on practice |
| **Growth** | Improving through experimentation and feedback |

## Areas of Focus

We explore different areas of software and technology, including:

- **Web Development** — Responsive websites and full-stack applications.
- **Software Engineering** — Practical applications, backend systems, and developer tools.
- **AI & Emerging Technologies** — Experimenting with artificial intelligence and modern technologies.
- **Game Development** — Exploring interactive experiences and game programming.
- **Open Source** — Learning collaborative development and contributing to shared projects.

## Projects

We aim to build and maintain projects that demonstrate practical development, teamwork, and problem-solving.

As projects are published, their repositories will serve as the source of truth for:

- Project overview and objectives
- Technologies and architecture
- Setup and installation instructions
- Features and development progress
- Contribution guidelines

Explore our [repositories](https://github.com/GX-Aniket-Labs?tab=repositories) to discover our work.

## Collaboration & Contributions

We welcome students and developers interested in learning, building, and collaborating.

You can get involved by:

1. Exploring our public repositories.
2. Finding a project that matches your interests.
3. Sharing suggestions or reporting issues.
4. Contributing improvements through pull requests.
5. Collaborating with other developers.

Before contributing, review the README and contribution instructions in the relevant repository.
